# ClauseProof Claims Boundary

## Required disclosure

> ClauseProof tests modeled commercial behavior in a synthetic agreement. It does not predict a court ruling, determine enforceability, or provide legal advice.

## Permitted description

> ClauseProof is a WebMCP-native contract testbench where a browser agent stages structured readings, the page deterministically executes them against visible scenarios, and the person defines the expected outcomes a proposed clarification must pass.

## Prohibited claims

Do not claim that ClauseProof provides legal advice, predicts courts, determines enforceability or legal correctness, performs formal verification, proves real-world truth, authorizes production use, invented executable contracts, or makes language unambiguous in every context.

Fingerprints are stale-state and demo-integrity checks only. A passing suite shows that a proposal satisfies the modeled behavior and boundaries selected for this synthetic case; it is not a legal conclusion.
